Curriculum

Four weeks.
Seven modules.

Each week builds directly on the last. By the end you will hear chord progressions, recognize scales, and understand why songs feel the way they do.

01
Week 1

The Foundation

Learn the 12 notes of the musical alphabet and exactly where they live on your fretboard. Map the chromatic scale across all six strings. Build the mental model everything else depends on.

Milestone: identify any note on any string instantly
02
Week 2

Intervals and Scales

Understand the distance between notes. Learn the major and minor scale patterns. These two patterns underpin every song ever written in Western music.

Milestone: play a major scale on any root note
03
Week 3

Chords and Harmony

Build major and minor chords from scratch using intervals. Understand why certain chords naturally want to follow others. Learn the progressions behind thousands of songs.

Milestone: analyze any song's chord progression
04
Week 4

Mastery and Beyond

Read basic sheet music and chord charts. Analyze real songs step by step. Pass the final assessment. Apply everything you have learned to actual playing.

Milestone: pass the music theory assessment
