Curriculum

Your 4-Week Journey

Each week builds on the last. By the end, you'll hear music differently.

Week 1 🎵

The Foundation

Learn the 12 notes of the musical alphabet and exactly where they live on your fretboard. By Friday you can identify any note on any string — instantly.

Week 2 📏

Intervals & Scales

Understand the distance between notes, then use that knowledge to build major and minor scales. Every song ever written lives inside these patterns.

Week 3 🎼

Chords & Harmony

Build major and minor chords from scratch. Learn which chords naturally want to follow others. Analyze why your favorite songs feel the way they do.

Week 4 🚀

Mastery & Beyond

Read sheet music, analyze song structure, and tie everything together. Final milestone: pass the music theory assessment and understand any song you hear.

Methodology

Built for guitar players, not music students

Traditional music theory is taught in a vacuum. This bootcamp connects every concept directly to tab, frets, and chord shapes you already know.

  • 🎸

    Tab-First Approach

    Every concept connects to guitar tablature you already understand — no piano required.

  • 👁️

    Visual Diagrams

    Every concept has a fretboard diagram or visual. See it, understand it, play it.

  • ⚡

    15–30 Min Lessons

    Bite-sized lessons that fit into real life. No marathon study sessions needed.

  • ✅

    Practice-Heavy

    50+ exercises with instant answers. Theory only sticks when you apply it.
