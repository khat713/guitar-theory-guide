Your 4-Week Path

Week 1

The Foundation

Learn the 12 notes, the musical alphabet, and where everything is on the fretboard. By Friday: you can identify any note on the fretboard.

Week 2

Intervals & Scales

Understand the distance between notes. Learn major and minor scales. By Friday: you can play a major scale and understand its pattern.

Week 3

Chords & Harmony

Learn how to build major and minor chords. Understand why certain chords go together. By Friday: you can analyze a song's chord progression.

Week 4

Mastery & Beyond

Read sheet music. Analyze songs. Apply theory to real music. Final test: pass the music theory assessment.
