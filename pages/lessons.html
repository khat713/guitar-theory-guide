THE
LESSONS

Start at Module 1. Each module gives you the building blocks the next one needs. Don't skip ahead.

01
Week 1

The 12 Notes

All of music uses exactly 12 different notes. Learn them in order, understand why they repeat, and find them on every string of your guitar.

20 min · Easy →
02
Week 1

The Fretboard Map

Your fretboard is a map of the 12 notes. Learn the pattern that repeats on every string, and identify any note anywhere in seconds.

25 min · Easy →
03
Week 2

Intervals

The distance between two notes is an interval. Intervals are the atoms of music theory: scales and chords are both just stacks of intervals.

30 min · Medium →
04
Week 2

Major and Minor Scales

A scale is an ordered set of notes following a specific interval pattern. Major sounds bright. Minor sounds dark. Every song lives inside one of these.

35 min · Medium →
05
Week 3

Chord Theory

Chords are built by stacking specific intervals. Learn to construct any major or minor chord from scratch, starting from any root note.

30 min · Medium →
06
Week 3

Chord Progressions

Certain chords naturally want to follow others. Learn the I-IV-V and I-V-vi-IV progressions behind thousands of songs, and understand the Roman numeral system.

25 min · Medium →
07
Week 4

Reading Music and Song Analysis

Read basic sheet music and chord charts. Analyze a real song from start to finish. Take the final assessment. You have earned it.

40 min · Advanced →
